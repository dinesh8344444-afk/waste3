AUTHENTIC INDUSTRIAL HERITAGE

A Family Business Built on Trust Since 1976

Gnanasekar Shell Lime Manufacture is a family business established in 1976. Started by the family's grandfather, the business continues its longstanding connection with the lime industry. We manufacture and supply shell lime products for customers with suitable construction, whitewashing, and other product-specific requirements.
